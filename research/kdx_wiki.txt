 Haxial KDX (software) 
 From Higher Intellect Vintage Wiki 
 The KDX software is a sequel of sorts to the Hotline protocol as it was also created by the same original developer.
 As of 2019 there are still a handful of KDX servers and trackers still in operation. You can also connect to the official preterhuman.net KDX server using URL kdx.preterhuman.net.
